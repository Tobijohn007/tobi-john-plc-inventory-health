Summary and what to do

Tobi John PLC holds ₦254.4bn of inventory, down 2.7% on last month and up 6.3% on a year ago. That is 11.4% of annual sales, or 53 days of stock (54 a year ago).

Of 25 business units, 2 are above their range, 0 below it and 23 in range. In total ₦6.4bn sits above range, costing about ₦132m a month to hold. The largest is Pasta, at ₦3.2bn above the top of its range.

250 A items (25% of items) make up 81% of usage value and hold 60% of inventory value, so planning effort should start there.

40 items have not moved for months and hold ₦7.4bn. 13 items may expire before use, with ₦3.1bn at risk.

As reported, inventory differs from the finance balance by -0.3%. After cleaning, the gap is -0.5%. 12 data errors worth ₦7.1bn were corrected this month. The reported figure looked closer to finance only because errors partly cancelled each other out. The remaining gap looks like timing, such as receipts not yet posted, and should be checked with finance.

What to do
  • Hold back purchasing in Pasta, Bran & Co-products until stock falls back into range.
  • Review the slow-moving items, starting with A items, and decide whether to sell, return or write them down.
  • Use at-risk items first, and agree a promotion or transfer for stock that cannot be used in time.
  • Agree with finance how to close the remaining gap, and use one inventory number in S&OP.
